Our Story

From classroom
hallways to
digital stages.

Cool Flow emerged in 2023 from the halls of Rosemont Hills Montessori College in Danao City, Cebu. What started as casual jam sessions between three friends — Bernard, Tristan, and Franklin — evolved into a dedicated acoustic collective.

We believe in the "one-take" philosophy — capturing raw, unfiltered performances that honor the original spirit of every song we cover. Our first major milestone came on December 31, 2023 with our "2023 Recap" video, compiling a year of musical growth and dedication.
